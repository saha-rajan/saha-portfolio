# About Saha Rajan

Sources: Current Resume + src/app/pages/About.tsx

## Background
Saha Rajan is a UX Designer and HCI graduate student (Arizona State University) experienced in B2B SaaS, web tools, and educational experiences. 

His journey began in architecture, designing physical spaces. Saha transitioned to product design ("traded concrete for code"), realizing that the principles of physical architecture—structure, circulation, and materiality—apply directly to digital environments.

## Core Design Philosophy
Building functional, human-centric systems. The core mission remains the same whether designing physical or digital spaces: translating user needs and requirements into clear, consistent digital experiences and engineering-ready specifications through collaborative workflows.

## Architecture Lessons Applied to Digital Design
* **Structural Integrity**: Just like a building needs a strong foundation, an app needs a robust information architecture.
* **Circulation & Flow**: In architecture we plan paths. In product design, we map user flows. The goal is the same: intuitive navigation.
* **Measure Twice**: Precision matters. The difference between good and great design often lies in the smallest details.
