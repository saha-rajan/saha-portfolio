# The Studio

Sources: src/app/pages/StudioDetail.tsx + src/app/components/Studio.tsx

## Overview
The Studio (route `/studio`) is a section of this portfolio — not a business or agency — where Saha's graphic design and creative work lives, alongside the product case studies. It is a collection of graphic design, campaign visuals, brand explorations, cinematic storytelling, and creative experiments: 18 works across three chapters.

It shows that Saha works as a **graphic designer** as well as a product / UX designer: brand identity, illustration and key art, print and information design, merchandise, wayfinding, motion design, 3D, and film.

## How to explore it
* **Homepage window**: On the homepage, the Studio section is a live window showing the Studio's thread canvas, slightly zoomed out. Hovering shows an "Enter the Studio" cursor; clicking grows the window to full screen while zooming in to the start of the thread, where the visitor can begin scrolling.
* **Grid view**: A flat gallery wall. Every piece is shown at its full shape with no cropping, grouped into the three chapters. Hovering a piece shows its title and tags; clicking opens it full screen.
* **Thread view (default)**: An interactive, scroll-driven journey. All works are scattered on a canvas and connected by a winding thread. Scrolling moves the camera along the thread: each piece opens as you reach it, with its description sliding in beside it, and closes as you move on (scrolling back reverses it). Chapter cards mark the move from Graphic & Print to 3D & Interactive to Motion & Film. At the end the camera pulls back to show the whole canvas, and scrolling further loops back to the start.
  * Print pieces animate as you scroll: the trifold brochure folds open, the infographic flips from front to back, and the wayfinding sheets shuffle one by one.
  * The Future Fab Heroes logo lifts off the logo design and lands exactly on the logo in the key art, then on the infographic.
* A **Thread / Grid** switch at the top centre swaps between the two. `/studio` opens the Thread view; the Grid view can be linked directly with `/studio?view=grid`.

## Chapter 01 — Graphic & Print
Brand identities, illustrations, print, merchandise, and visual systems built across real projects.

1. **Future Fab Heroes Logo** (Adobe Illustrator · Brand Identity) — Designed the visual identity for Future Fab Heroes, turning Chip-E and semiconductor circuitry into a recognizable game mark.
2. **Future Fab Heroes Key Art** (Adobe Illustrator · Key Art) — Extended the game identity into illustrated key art, building a Mars-inspired world around Chip-E for promotional use.
3. **Future Fab Heroes Infographic** (Print Design · Information Design) — Designed a two-sided infographic that introduces the game, its Learn · Build · Defend framework, target audiences, and Steam access.
4. **Future Fab Heroes Print Flyer** (Print Design · Collateral) — Took the digital game identity into print, creating a physical flyer for outreach, events, and student engagement.
5. **Flyer Layout & Mockup** (Print Design · Mockup) — Built the front-and-back flyer as one cohesive print system and visualized how the final piece works as a physical handout.
6. **Sticker in the Wild** (Merchandise · Brand Application) — Tested the identity beyond screens through physical vinyl applications, bringing the Future Fab Heroes brand into everyday objects.
7. **TalentVault Logo Animation** (After Effects · Motion Design) — Brought the TalentVault mark to life through a short motion study exploring how the identity can form, move, and resolve.
8. **Workforce Development Trifold** (Print Design · Information Design · ASU SWAP Hub) — Designed a workforce development brochure for ASU SWAP Hub, organizing academic pathways, micro-credentials, digital learning, and industry opportunities into a clear print system.
9. **Campus Wayfinding Sheets** (Wayfinding · Information Design · Event Collateral) — Created event wayfinding sheets for multiple ASU venues (ISTB 12, ASU Chandler Innovation Center, Cooley Ballroom), combining parking, entrances, landmarks, and 3D location visuals to make arrival easier for attendees.

## Chapter 02 — 3D & Interactive
10. **Gesture-Controlled Mandala** (MediaPipe · WebGL · Creative Coding) — Built a webcam-based interaction where hand movement controls a Doctor Strange-inspired generative mandala in real time. Visitors can launch and try it in the Studio.
11. **Interactive 3D Character** (Spline · 3D · Interactive) — Modeled a playful robot in Spline and turned it into an interactive web-based 3D experiment exploring character, lighting, and spatial interaction.
12. **VR Lobby Environment** (3D Design · VR Environment) — Designed a lobby environment as an entry space for an experimental VR experience, exploring spatial storytelling and navigation in an immersive setting.
13. **Vibe-Coded 3D Environment** (Blender · Python · Gemini · Antigravity) — Built a 3D semiconductor-inspired environment in Blender through a code-driven workflow using Gemini Antigravity and Python, with the model generated without manual modeling.
