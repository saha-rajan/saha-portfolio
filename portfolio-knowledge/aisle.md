# Case Study: AIsle

## Project Metadata
* **Project Name**: AIsle — AI knowledge management
* **Role**: Product Designer, Concept & Product Strategy
* **Team**: Saha + Deepika (2-person team)
* **Timeline**: 1 semester
* **Project Type**: Academic project, Web-based prototype

## Overview
AIsle is a concept for the "missing layer" in multi-agent AI workflows. It is a cross-platform knowledge management system designed to help users organize, retrieve, and resume conversations across multiple AI tools. Instead of organizing work around individual AI platforms (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot), AIsle groups AI conversations and outputs around the central project they belong to.

## Key Features & Design Concepts
1. **Retrieve**: "Search across the work, not the tool." Allows users to search through connected AI conversation histories centrally.
2. **Resume**: "Returning to work shouldn’t mean rebuilding the context." AIsle restores the project state so users can seamlessly continue their workflow.
3. **Trace**: "Make AI contribution visible." The system keeps AI contributions connected to project outputs, creating an audit trail.

## Research
### Completed Research (Affinity Mapping)
* **Workflow**: Users spread work across tools resulting in too many tools to manage.
* **Retrieval**: Searching past chats takes too long.
* **Documentation**: Manual tracking of AI prompts/decisions is fragile.
* **Accountability**: Lack of audit trails makes it hard to cite AI work or trace reasoning.

### Proposed / Future Research
* **Hypotheses to Validate**: A larger 24-participant mixed-methods study has been *proposed* (but not yet completed) to evaluate future research hypotheses, including retrieval performance over large datasets and broader synchronization support.
