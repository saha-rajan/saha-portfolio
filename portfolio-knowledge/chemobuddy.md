## Overview
ChemoBuddy is a digital healthcare platform focused on creating a patient-centered education platform. The platform delivers personalized education, real-time guidance, and user-friendly tracking capabilities to help patients stay informed and confident throughout their treatment journey.

## Core Experience / Final Solutions
1. **Command Center Dashboard**: A centralized, calm hub designed to reduce cognitive burden. It prioritizes relevant daily actions, upcoming schedules, and immediate symptom-logging access without overwhelming the patient.
2. **Onboarding**: Goal is to create immediate safety and trust. Uses secure clinic code authentication, automatic import of treatment schedules, and progressive feature introduction.
3. **Role-Play Research**: Conducted structured role-play scenarios (e.g., New Diagnosis, Mid-Treatment Management) with a clinical oncology mentor to simulate patient behaviors and questions.
