# Case Study: Aura

## Project Metadata
* **Project Name**: Aura
* **Timeline**: 48 hours
* **Project Type**: Speculative product design
* **Focus**: Workplace social awareness & emotional presence

## Overview
Aura is a speculative workplace presence concept designed around the idea: "Feel the room before you approach." It explores whether digital products can restore the subtle social awareness humans naturally use before approaching a colleague, preventing the collision of incompatible contexts (e.g., interrupting someone who is deeply stressed or focused).

## Core Concepts & Experience
* **Context Over Certainty**: Aura does not claim to definitively tell you how someone feels. Instead, it helps communicate the context they choose to share.
* **Decide How to Approach**: Aura translates shared moments into considerate next steps, guiding whether someone should approach now, give their colleague space, or connect later.
* **Beyond the Moment**: The system extends awareness and agency across setup, self-reflection, and visibility.

## Ethics & Consent
Presence systems only work when users stay in control. Aura is designed so that the system can suggest a state, but the person always decides what becomes visible to others. It includes features like:
* Ghost Mode
* Confirm / Override state suggestions
* Broadcasting rules
